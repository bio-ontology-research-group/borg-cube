# Postdoc addendum to the mentoring compact

Attach to `compact-template.md` for postdoctoral researchers. A postdoc is an employee in training for independence, not a student; the compact therefore covers the position, the independence path and the exit, and the IDP is career-led.

## Position

- Contract dates, funding source and the date by which renewal or non-renewal is decided (at least <six> months before the end).
- Percentage of time on the funded project versus own research direction: <e.g. 70/30>, revisited yearly.
- Supervision of students: which students, what the postdoc is responsible for, and that Robert remains the supervisor of record.

## Path to independence

- Own research line: the postdoc proposes one within the first <six> months; Robert commits to support it with compute, data and co-authorship discussions.
- Fellowship and grant applications: at least one per year is expected and supported (grant-writing skill when available); the postdoc is PI or named researcher where the funder allows.
- Senior authorship: on papers driven by the postdoc's own line, the postdoc can be corresponding or last author, agreed at the outline stage.
- Visibility: one invited or conference talk per year; membership in a programme committee or review panel encouraged.

## Mentoring the postdoc

- 1:1 every <two weeks>, career check every <three months>: what has moved toward the next position, what is missing, who to talk to.
- Mentors outside the group: at least one, chosen by the postdoc.
- Teaching: a guest lecture or a course module per year if the postdoc wants a teaching record; not more than <N> hours per week.

## Exit

- Twelve months before the contract ends: written plan for the job search, the remaining papers, and the handover of code, data and students.
- Reference letters: Robert writes them on request; the postdoc gives two weeks' notice and the job description.
- Handover checklist: repositories documented and transferred to the organisation, data with README, unfinished manuscripts with an agreed author list and a named person who finishes each.
