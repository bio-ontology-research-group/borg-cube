# Contributor roles (CRediT) and the contributions table

The 14 CRediT roles (ANSI/NISO Z39.104-2022, CC-BY 4.0, https://credit.niso.org/)
and the short descriptors we use. Authorship itself follows the ICMJE criteria
(substantial contribution, drafting or critical revision, final approval,
accountability); CRediT records what each author did, it does not decide who
is an author. Evidence and rules: `references/paper-writing.md` and
`references/reporting-standards.md`.

| Role | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| Conceptualization | ideas, research goals and aims |
| Data curation | annotation, cleaning, maintaining data for use and reuse |
| Formal analysis | statistical, mathematical or computational analysis of data |
| Funding acquisition | acquiring the financial support for the project |
| Investigation | conducting the research and experiments, collecting data or evidence |
| Methodology | design of the methodology, creation of models |
| Project administration | coordination and management of the research activity |
| Resources | providing materials, data, software, compute, instruments |
| Software | programming, implementation, testing of code, supporting algorithms |
| Supervision | oversight and leadership, mentorship of the core team |
| Validation | verification of reproducibility of results and other outputs |
| Visualization | preparation and presentation of figures and data visualizations |
| Writing, original draft | preparation and writing of the initial draft |
| Writing, review and editing | critical review, commentary, revision at any stage |

## Table template

One row per author, filled at the planning stage and updated before every
submission. Lead means primarily responsible, equal means shared equally,
supporting means contributed. Leave a cell empty when the author did not take
that role. The author order statement and the acknowledgments of non-author
contributors go below the table.

## Statement wording

Author contributions: A.B.: conceptualization, methodology, software,
investigation, formal analysis, visualization, writing (original draft). C.D.:
data curation, validation, writing (review and editing). R.H.: conceptualization,
supervision, funding acquisition, writing (review and editing). All authors
read and approved the final manuscript.
