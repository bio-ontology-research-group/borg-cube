## What the evidence says

### Scope and argument

- A final-year plan should make priorities, required administration, writing, and career work visible, with regular review and enough flexibility for unexpected work. [marino2014]
- Starting to write after the agreed essential experiments are complete prevents the pursuit of a perfect but indefinitely delayed thesis. [marino2014, phillips-pugh-how-to-get-a-phd]
- A thesis is evidence of competent independent research training and an argument rather than a chronological report or a claim to a masterpiece. [phillips-pugh-how-to-get-a-phd]
- A clear research document centers one contribution, keeps a reader who does not know the work in view, and uses context, content, and conclusion at document and paragraph scale. [mensh2017]
- Results chapters should lead with a claim and follow it with evidence, while a conclusion states what was learned and what remains open. [zobel-writing-for-computer-science]
- A literature review evaluates and connects prior work; it does not merely list sources. [zobel-writing-for-computer-science]

### Drafting and chapter evidence

- An outline and a logical sequence of figures and tables can guide both the research story and the writing, while figures and tables should be self-contained and interpreted in prose rather than repeated. [zhang2014]
- Complete drafts need several revision rounds, including a reader-oriented self-review that tests logic, results, organization, and presentation. [zhang2014, phillips-pugh-how-to-get-a-phd]
- Technical terms and acronyms should be defined for a reader unfamiliar with the work, and consistent wording reduces unnecessary reader effort. [mensh2017]
- Cite only work that has been read and that supports the specific claim; check each reference against its original source. [zobel-writing-for-computer-science]
- Every figure and table needs a text reference and self-contained caption, while symbols need definition before use and notation must remain consistent. [zobel-writing-for-computer-science]

### Defense preparation

- Practice talks and feedback before a defense help expose questions and improve confidence. [marino2014, bourne2007-oral]
- A presentation should serve its audience, use a clear story, and rehearse enough that the presenter can adapt rather than read slides. [bourne2007-oral, naegle2021]
- Slides should have a single takeaway, legible visual hierarchy, and figures that support rather than duplicate the spoken argument. [naegle2021]
- A computational MS thesis benefits from a written plan, a bounded question, early communication with the supervisor, and a documented reproducible workflow. [pcbi-2025-msc-thesis]

### KAUST boundary

- The Registrar guide requires committee approval by the Dean and specifies dissertation committees of four to five members and MS thesis committees of three to four members. [kaust-registrar-program-guide]
- The Registrar guide directs students to the Academic Calendar for petition, result, and archiving deadlines, so a local planner cannot supply those calendar dates without a dated official record. [kaust-registrar-program-guide]
- The local thesis-policy record identifies the KAUST thesis guidelines and LaTeX template as proprietary and not fetched, so the skill must not reproduce template details or claim a requirement that has not been reverified. [kaust-graduate-affairs-thesis-policy]
- The local CEMSE milestone page requires PhD defense-announcement material at least two weeks before the defense and directs students to the current official process. [kaust-cemse-milestones]

## Rules we adopt

1. State one thesis-level contribution, then give every chapter a claim, evidence, and role in the thesis argument. (from [mensh2017], [phillips-pugh-how-to-get-a-phd])
2. Make a chapter-to-paper map before drafting. Mark each paper's status and explain its thesis role, including synthesis chapters that have no paper. (from [zhang2014], [pcbi-2025-msc-thesis])
3. Draft results chapters before every remaining experiment is complete when the agreed minimum evidence exists, and revise each chapter through feedback rounds. (from [marino2014], [phillips-pugh-how-to-get-a-phd], [zhang2014])
4. Make related work position the contribution against prior work. Cite the exact source of every factual or methodological claim. (from [zobel-writing-for-computer-science])
5. Reference each figure and table in prose, define acronyms and notation before use, and keep their names stable across the chapter. (from [mensh2017], [zobel-writing-for-computer-science])
6. Schedule chapter drafts, full draft, revision rounds, formatting check, and committee copy backward from the intended defense, but label the writing dates as internal targets. (from [marino2014], [zhang2014])
7. Use only a KAUST date or rule that carries its guideline source and verified-on date. Treat any Academic Calendar deadline missing from the local record as unresolved. (from [kaust-registrar-program-guide], [kaust-graduate-affairs-thesis-policy])
8. Rehearse a defense story for the audience and solicit questions before Robert approves material for sharing. (from [marino2014], [bourne2007-oral], [naegle2021])

## Where sources disagree

- Completion standard: Marino warns against pursuing the perfect thesis, while a structured-paper account stresses thorough evidence and presentation. We use a bounded, defensible contribution with adequate evidence, rather than either perfectionism or an unsupported short cut. [marino2014, zhang2014]
- Committee-delivery window: the local CEMSE page states three weeks for an MS thesis, while the local KAUST thesis-policy record used by this skill states a six-week delivery window. The planner preserves the six-week window as the stricter local preparation rule and requires rechecking the current guideline before action. [kaust-cemse-milestones, kaust-graduate-affairs-thesis-policy]
- Source availability: the Phillips and Pugh and Zobel book notes provide local summaries, while local notes for Dunleavy and Evans, Gruba, and Zobel are absent. This reference relies on the available summaries and does not attribute a specific rule to an unavailable note. [phillips-pugh-how-to-get-a-phd, zobel-writing-for-computer-science, dunleavy2003, evans-gruba-zobel-better-thesis]
