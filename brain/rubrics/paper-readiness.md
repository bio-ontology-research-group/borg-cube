# Rubric: paper readiness

Applied by the editor before submission; two independent reviews are merged
into one verdict `submit|revise|hold`. Each item: pass, fix (with location),
or blocker.

| Item | Test | Grounding |
|---|---|---|
| One message | the paper's contribution fits one sentence and the title and abstract state it | Mensh and Kording 2017 (ten simple rules for structuring papers) |
| Structure | context-content-conclusion at paper, section and paragraph level | Mensh and Kording 2017; Zhang 2014 (ten simple rules for writing research papers) |
| Claims versus evidence | every claim in abstract and discussion points to a figure, table or statistic | Bourne 2005 (getting published); Frassl et al. 2018 |
| Figures | each figure makes one point, captions are self-contained and checked against the plotted data | Rougier, Droettboom and Bourne 2014 (better figures); Weissgerber et al. 2015 (beyond bar and line graphs) |
| Sentence-level clarity | old before new, stress position, subjects near verbs | Gopen and Swan 1990 (the science of scientific writing) |
| Methods reproducibility | data, code, versions, seeds, parameters, availability statements | Sandve et al. 2013; REFORMS 2024 (Kapoor et al.); DOME 2021 for ML |
| References | every DOI resolves and matches title and authors; no orphan citations | corpus-verify Crossref check; Noble 2017 (response to reviewers, for revisions) |
| Authorship and credit | ICMJE criteria met; CRediT roles recorded; ORCIDs present | ICMJE recommendations; CRediT taxonomy |
| Reporting standards | domain checklist (MIRO for ontologies, DOME for ML, NeurIPS checklist where relevant) | Matentzoglu et al. 2018; Walsh et al. 2021; NeurIPS paper checklist |
| Venue fit | scope, length, format, preprint policy, open-access route | Romano and Nolte 2020 (choosing a venue) |
| Limitations | stated honestly, not buried | Frassl et al. 2018 |
| Revision (if resubmission) | point-by-point table, every reviewer comment answered, change log | Noble 2017; Bourne and Korngreen 2006 |

A blocker on claims-versus-evidence, references or reproducibility gives
`hold`. The verdict is Robert-only until he releases it.
