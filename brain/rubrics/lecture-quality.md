# Rubric: lecture quality

Applied by the group leader to a lecture or course produced by the lecturer.
Each row: meets, partial, missing, with slide or section numbers.

| Row | Criterion | Grounding |
|---|---|---|
| Outcomes | stated with Bloom verbs, observable, each assessed somewhere | Anderson and Krathwohl 2001; Biggs 1996 (constructive alignment) |
| Backward design | assessments designed before activities; alignment matrix complete | Wiggins and McTighe (Understanding by Design); Fink 2003 self-directed guide |
| Active learning | at least one active segment per 15 to 20 minutes with a formative check | Freeman et al. 2014 (PNAS); Deslauriers et al. 2019 (PNAS); Theobald et al. 2020 (PNAS); Wieman 2014 |
| Cognitive load | one idea per slide, no redundant text read aloud, signalling used | Mayer (multimedia learning principles); Mayer and Moreno 2003 |
| Slide design | statement titles, assertion-evidence layout, sentence case, verified captions, no filler | Alley (Craft of Scientific Presentations); Garner and Alley 2013; the presentation skill |
| Retrieval and spacing | practice questions, spaced review, low-stakes quizzes | Dunlosky et al. 2013; Brown, Roediger and McDaniel (Make It Stick) |
| Inclusion and access | materials readable, alternatives for figures, pace and language checked | CAST Universal Design for Learning; Carpentries Instructor Training |
| Evidence base | claims and figures sourced; data current | NAP 2018 (How People Learn II); Brown and Wilson 2018 (ten quick tips for teaching programming) |
| Teaching programming (where relevant) | live coding, worked examples, faded scaffolding, pair work | Wilson (Teaching Tech Together); Carpentries |
| Logistics | timebox holds, KAUST registrar syllabus rules met, materials FAIR | KAUST registrar course rules; Garcia et al. 2020 (FAIR training materials) |

A lecture with unassessed outcomes or unverified figure captions is
returned to the lecturer. Robert releases anything that reaches students.
