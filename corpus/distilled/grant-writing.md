## What the evidence says

- A proposal should offer a novel idea while avoiding a level of speculation that makes the work implausible, and it should connect appropriate preliminary results to the specific aims and the team's ability to execute them [bourne-chalupa2006].
- A call must be read closely and its stated submission requirements, including supporting material such as budget justification, must be followed because noncompliance can prevent substantive review [bourne-chalupa2006].
- A concise proposal can still be complete when it gives reviewers a realistic scope, makes key points easy to find, and does not depend on material outside the allowed submission [bourne-chalupa2006].
- Internal review needs time before the deadline, and reviewers benefit when the proposal is understandable to both experts and nonexperts [bourne-chalupa2006].
- The Heilmeier Catechism asks for the objective without jargon, the current approach and its limits, the proposed novelty and basis for success, the value of success, risks, cost, duration, and mid-term and final tests of success [heilmeier-catechism].
- The supplied ERC page identifies grant schemes and directs applicants to information about assessment, but its stored text does not state evaluation criteria, so no ERC criterion should be inferred from it [erc-evaluation-criteria].
- The NIH application guide is listed in the corpus manifest but its source text was not fetched; any NIH-specific requirement remains unverified until checked against the current application materials [nih-application-guide].
- The HHMI/BWF guide is listed in the corpus manifest but no source text or book note is available here; any claim attributed to it must be verified before it drives a decision [hhmi-bwf-making-the-right-moves].
- KAUST internal calls are listed in the corpus manifest without fetched call text, so their current eligibility, deadline, format, and annex requirements must be copied from the actual call and confirmed by Robert [kaust-internal-calls].

## Rules we adopt

1. Transcribe the current call's requirements into a dated call definition and treat that source, not this skill or a remembered rule, as the authority. Mark unverified entries for Robert to confirm. (from [bourne-chalupa2006], [kaust-internal-calls], [nih-application-guide])
2. State the gap, the central objective, the approach, the expected difference, and why the team can do the work in the one-page summary. (from [bourne-chalupa2006], [heilmeier-catechism])
3. Write each aim with a measurable success criterion, feasibility evidence, a material risk, and a fallback or mitigation. Avoid aims whose value depends on a previous aim succeeding. (from [bourne-chalupa2006], [heilmeier-catechism])
4. Map every aim to milestones, deliverables, an owner, a decision point, and a budget assumption before drafting the work plan. (from [heilmeier-catechism], [bourne-chalupa2006])
5. Make the budget narrative explain why each requested resource is needed for a stated activity, deliverable, or risk control. Do not request resources that the work plan cannot justify. (from [heilmeier-catechism], [bourne-chalupa2006])
6. Draft for a reviewer who may not be the narrowest specialist: lead with the point of each section, use plain language, and keep the scope realistic. (from [bourne-chalupa2006])
7. Run the deterministic call checklist and aims lint before internal review, then resolve or explicitly retain every finding with evidence. (from [bourne-chalupa2006], [heilmeier-catechism])

## Where sources disagree

- General grant advice can recommend a realistic, reviewer-friendly proposal, while a particular call can impose a different structure or evidence threshold; the current call wins because it governs eligibility and submission [bourne-chalupa2006, kaust-internal-calls, nih-application-guide].
- The supplied ERC page points to assessment information but does not expose the criteria in the stored text, whereas the Heilmeier Catechism gives a general project-question framework; use the Catechism for internal thinking and verify the current scheme criteria before claiming alignment [erc-evaluation-criteria, heilmeier-catechism].
